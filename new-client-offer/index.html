For new Brightyard clients

Your first maintenance visit is free. The second is half off.

Hiring a new landscaper means trusting someone you haven't seen work yet. So for new clients who start regular maintenance with us, the first visit is on us and the second is half price. You see the work before you pay full price for it.

By Joaquin, owner of Brightyard · Last reviewed November 16, 2026

The short version

  • Who it's for: new clients starting regular maintenance (weekly, every other week, or monthly) at a home or managed property in our service area.
  • What you get: your first regular maintenance visit free, and your second at half the quoted price. Visits after that are at the regular price on your quote.
  • How to claim it: text or call (747) 239-4613, or request a call, and mention the new client offer. We walk the property and give you a written quote first.
  • Fine print: one per property, not combined with other offers. One-time cleanups and extra work are quoted separately.

Step by step

How it works

  1. Reach out. Text or call (747) 239-4613, or use the request form. A few photos of the yard help us get ready.
  2. Walkthrough. We walk the property with you and talk through what you want kept up and how often.
  3. Written quote. You get a per-visit price and a schedule. If the yard needs a one-time cleanup before regular maintenance makes sense, we quote that separately so you can see both.
  4. First visit: free.
  5. Second visit: half off the per-visit price on your quote.
  6. From the third visit on: the regular price on your quote.
Included

What a maintenance visit includes

Every property is different, so the exact list goes on your quote. A typical visit covers:

  • Lawn care: mowing and edging.
  • Beds: weeding and tidying.
  • Hedges and shrubs: touch-up trimming to keep their shape.
  • Hard surfaces: clearing clippings and leaves off walks, driveways, and patios.
  • Irrigation: a quick look for broken heads and leaks, and keeping the controller on your watering days.
  • Green waste: hauled away.
Quoted separately

What isn't part of the offer

These are separate jobs with their own quote. We're happy to price any of them alongside your maintenance plan.

  • A one-time cleanup to bring an overgrown yard back to a maintainable state
  • Tree trimming
  • Removing shrubs or plants, like an old hibiscus
  • Irrigation repairs that need parts
  • Planting and garden refreshes
  • Hauling away furniture, junk, or other non-yard items
Eligibility

Who it's for

The offer is for properties we haven't worked on before: homes, rentals, HOAs, apartment buildings, and small commercial properties. Property managers can use it once for each property they bring on.

We work across the San Fernando Valley and Ventura County, including Calabasas, Hidden Hills, Woodland Hills, Tarzana, Encino, Sherman Oaks, Studio City, Agoura Hills, Westlake Village, Thousand Oaks, Porter Ranch, and Granada Hills.

Terms

The fine print

  • For new clients only, one per property.
  • Applies when you start a regular maintenance plan: weekly, every other week, or monthly.
  • The free and half-off visits are your first two regular maintenance visits, priced at the per-visit rate on your written quote.
  • Doesn't apply to one-time cleanups, tree work, removals, repairs, planting, or haul-away.
  • Can't be combined with other Brightyard offers.
  • Available while this page is posted. If we end the offer, any plan already agreed to keeps it.
FAQ

Common questions

Is the first visit really free?

Yes. Your first regular maintenance visit costs nothing. The second is half the per-visit price on your quote, and visits after that are at the regular price.

Do I have to sign up for regular service?

Yes. The offer is for new clients starting a regular maintenance plan, weekly, every other week, or monthly. It doesn't apply to one-time jobs.

My yard is overgrown. Does the free visit cover the cleanup?

No. If the yard needs a one-time cleanup before regular maintenance makes sense, we quote it separately. The free and half-off visits are your first two regular visits after that.

Does the offer work for rentals, HOAs, and commercial properties?

Yes, for properties we haven't worked on before, including homes, rentals, HOAs, apartment buildings, and small commercial properties. Property managers can use it once for each property.

How do I claim the offer?

Text or call (747) 239-4613, or use the request form on our homepage, and mention the new client offer. We walk the property and give you a written quote before any work starts.

Can I combine this with another Brightyard offer?

No. It's one offer per property.
